About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery is imagined as a welcoming neighborhood gathering place, where familiar faces and fresh baking are part of the daily routine.

Our goal is to create a comfortable place where customers can enjoy fresh breads, pastries, and cakes made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We aim to use thoughtfully selected ingredients and prepare our baked goods in small batches.

We believe that quality ingredients and careful preparation help create delicious baked goods for our customers.

Customers with questions about ingredients or food allergies are encouraged to contact our bakery team.

Meet the Team

Our bakers prepare breads, pastries, and specialty cakes for the neighborhood.

Our counter team welcomes customers, answers questions about our products, and helps plan preorders for special occasions.
